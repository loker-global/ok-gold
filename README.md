# OK.GOLD

**A shared domain for builders who ship.**

OK.GOLD is Ethereum-native infrastructure that enables developers and creators to claim subdomains using their wallets—no gatekeepers, no platforms, just you, your keys, and the things you make.

## 🌟 Features

- **Wallet-First**: Your wallet is your account. Your keys are your authority.
- **Decentralized**: Smart contracts, not databases. Immutable ownership records.
- **Privacy-First**: No surveillance. No tracking. No data extraction.
- **Developer-Friendly**: Built for permanence, not quarterly growth.
- **Account Abstraction**: Wallets are first-class citizens.
- **Invisible Infrastructure**: The protocol fades into the background.

## 🚀 How It Works

### 1. **Intent**
You have something to build. A tool, a page, an experiment worth sharing.

### 2. **Name**  
Claim your subdomain. Your wallet is your account. Your keys are your authority.

### 3. **Live**
Ship it. Update it. Own it. No intermediaries between you and the web.
